categories: reasoning, evidence, alternatives, framing

- List the conclusions the work reaches. For each, does it follow from the evidence the work itself presents? Say which link in the chain is weak.
- Is the evidence adequate for the strength of the claim: sample size, comparability, cherry-picking, survivorship, stale or one-sided data?
- What alternative explanations or options fit the same evidence and are not considered?
- Are comparisons fair (like with like, same period, same basis)?
- Is the framing loaded: a question posed so only one answer fits, or options omitted?
- Are recommendations actionable and consistent with the analysis, and are the risks and uncertainties stated?
- Separate facts, estimates and opinions; flag where they are blurred.
