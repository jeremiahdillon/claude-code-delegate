- Verify only against the material on disk: the work and the reference material the brief lists. Do not fetch URLs or search the web.
- For each factual claim that matters: is it supported by a provided source? Quote the source passage. If no provided source supports it, report it as "not supported by the provided sources", not as false.
- Does each citation say what the work claims it says? Flag misquotes, overstatements, and claims attributed to the wrong source.
- Are there provided sources that contradict the work and are not addressed?
- Does the work cover what its question requires, or are important parts of the provided material left out?
- Note dates: is the work relying on material that is stale for the claim it makes?
