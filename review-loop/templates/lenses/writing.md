- What is the piece trying to achieve, and for whom (from the brief)? Judge it against that, not against a genre or style you would prefer.
- Is the central claim or message clear, and does the piece deliver on it? Point to where it drifts, overreaches or contradicts itself.
- Does each step of the argument follow? Name gaps, leaps, circular reasoning, and claims asserted rather than supported.
- Are the strongest counterarguments or objections handled, or ignored?
- Would the stated audience understand it? Flag undefined terms, missing context, and assumptions the reader can't share.
- Does the order help the reader? Flag sections that belong elsewhere, repetition, and buried conclusions.
- Quote the exact passage for every finding.
